Controlled test: 1000 RPM, no load
CH3: MCU NRST, 10X
CH4: MCU 3.3 V, 10X
ST-Link NRST: disconnected

Result:
- COM9 disappeared and recovered; stop was sent after recovery.
- scope state changed Ready -> Stop and both 1,000,000-point waveforms were saved.
- reboot diagnostic: boot=4, flags=0x08, reason=EXTERNAL_PIN.

CH3 / NRST:
- normal/top approximately 3.28 V
- captured minimum approximately 1.65 V
- negative pulse width approximately 1.04 us
- falling edge approximately 16 ns; rising edge approximately 2.58 us
- raw trigger window contains a sustained negative excursion down to -88 counts.

CH4 / 3.3 V:
- mean approximately 3.29 V
- contains high-frequency probe/common-mode spikes, so global min/max are not rail accuracy measurements
- at the NRST trigger there is no sustained rail collapse matching the CH3 pulse; raw samples alternate in polarity rather than remaining low

Conclusion:
- This is an NRST-network transient, not a USB-only software failure.
- The evidence does not show a sustained 3.3 V rail collapse at the reset event.
- Priority moves to board-level NRST immunity: local pull-up, RC filter, reset-supervisor/other NRST drivers, trace coupling, and short-ground probing.
